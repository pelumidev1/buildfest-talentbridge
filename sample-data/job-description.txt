Data Analyst, Commercial
TalentBridge Consulting, on behalf of a retail and payments client
Lagos, hybrid

About the role
The commercial team makes pricing, promotion and merchant decisions weekly and
currently waits on the engineering backlog for every number. We are hiring a
Data Analyst who will own that reporting end to end and become the person the
commercial leads bring questions to.

What you will do
- Build and maintain dashboards the commercial team uses to run the week
- Write and own the SQL models behind revenue, margin and churn reporting
- Design and read experiments on pricing and promotions
- Turn open questions from commercial leads into analysis with a recommendation

Requirements
- At least 3 years working as a data analyst or in a closely related analytics role
- Advanced SQL, including window functions and building reusable models
- Python for analysis, specifically pandas
- Power BI or Tableau, with dashboards you built yourself in production use
- Experience building reporting for commercial, sales or marketing stakeholders
  rather than for engineers
- Experience designing or analysing A/B tests or other controlled experiments
- A degree in a numerate discipline such as statistics, mathematics, economics,
  engineering or computer science

Nice to have
- dbt or a similar transformation framework
- Retail, e-commerce or payments domain experience
- Experience mentoring junior analysts

What we are not looking for
- Dashboard maintenance only, with no analysis
- Reporting built solely in spreadsheets
